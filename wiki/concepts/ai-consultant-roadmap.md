# AI Consultant Roadmap

## What it is

[[nate-herk]]'s *So You Learned Claude, Now What?* (32.4K views, 2026-06-22, 16:53) in [[youtube-digest-apify-2026-06-24]] — a career-direction framework for people who have built AI skills and don't know how to turn them into money.

The setup: there are many paths — *"start your own agency, you might already run a business, or you might just want to level up at the job you already have."* His thesis:

> **The path you pick matters less than the skills underneath it and the way you go about using them.**

## The structure

### Tools mean nothing (00:36)

> *"The tools keep changing but the real value sits with the person who can figure out what's actually worth building."*

The opening reframe and the spine of the whole video: tool fluency is not the asset. **Knowing what's worth building** is. ("Next wave?" 01:46; "Nobody's good [yet]" 02:42 — the field is early enough that judgment, not tenure, is the differentiator.)

### Doctor or pharmacist? (02:55) — the two roads (03:51)

The framework's signature split — **two roads into AI consulting**:

| Road | Analogy | What you sell |
|---|---|---|
| **Doctor** | Diagnostician | Decides *what* to build — judgment, problem-framing, "what's worth building" |
| **Pharmacist** | Dispenser | Implements a *known* fix — execution of a defined solution |

Both are viable; they're different businesses with different skill demands. (Echoes his earlier two-path framing — strategy/training vs done-for-you — sharpened into the doctor/pharmacist metaphor.)

### Six-figure seats (04:39) · Why hire you? (08:01) · Expiration date (10:43) · From zero (15:11)

- **Six-figure seats** — the roles/offers that actually pay well sit on the doctor side (judgment), not pure dispensing
- **Why hire you?** — the differentiation question; tool skills don't answer it, judgment does
- **Expiration date** — tool-specific skills *expire*; the durable layer is the skill of figuring out what to build (the explicit "tools mean nothing" payoff)
- **From zero** — a simple roadmap that works regardless of which road you pick

## Why it matters for 3Ps

- **The doctor/pharmacist split is a positioning tool** — 3Ps should sell as the *doctor* (decide what's worth building), explicitly above the commoditizing dispense layer.
- **"Tools expire, judgment doesn't"** is the durable-value pitch for any client or audience nervous about tool churn — and the rationale for selling *outcomes/judgment*, not tool training.
- **A clean audience-content format** — "you learned the tool, now what?" is the exact question the vault's target persona is asking; reusable as a 3Ps content angle.
