# Context Wars

## The thesis

[[nate-b-jones]]' framing (in [[youtube-digest-apify-2026-06-30]] #5, *The Real Story Behind the Government GPT 5.6 Freeze*, 32.1K, 17:13): the AI competition is moving **from the intelligence wars to the context wars.**

> *"The real story isn't which lab has the smartest model. It's who controls the context that makes any model useful: your files, your Slack, your phone."*

As **frontier intelligence slows** — and frontier *releases* are deliberately slowed (the US-government GPT-5.6 freeze) — raw capability stops being the differentiator. **The next real advantage is the context where your work actually lives**, and which lab can apply intelligence *to that context*.

## The three fronts (chapter map)

| Front | Player | Move |
|---|---|---|
| **Your phone** | Apple | Wiring **Siri into your personal/life context** (3:13) |
| **Your Slack** | [[anthropic]] | **[[claude-tag]]** lives inside Slack (5:44) |
| **Your work / sensitive tasks** | OpenAI | **[[codex]]** earning trust to handle sensitive work; how employees actually adopted it (8:29) |

Backdrop: **the US government slowing frontier releases** (12:51) — the GPT-5.6 "freeze" — which is *why* the edge shifts to context rather than capability.

## Why it matters for this wiki

- **The umbrella thesis over the vault's whole context cluster.** [[harness-over-model]] (the harness is the durable layer), [[glm]] (cheap model, but you can't move your context — the last mile), [[claude-tag]] (the Slack stickiness mechanism), [[open-engine]] (own the agent-handoff layer), [[karpathy-llm-wiki]] (own your data moat), [[ai-operating-system]] ("context is king") are all **specific moves inside the context wars**. This page is where they ladder up.
- **The demand-side mirror of [[harness-over-model]].** Where harness-over-model is the *operator/builder* prescription (architect for harness flexibility), context-wars is the *industry-structure* read (whoever owns the context wins the customer). [[glm]]'s "why can't companies switch?" (#4 same batch) is the two theses meeting: the switch is blocked because the **context** is trapped in the incumbent's harness.
- **Privacy as the hidden cost.** *"The payoff is real when AI finally knows your context, but the same access quietly turns every convenience into a decision about what you are willing to hand over."* The convenience/surveillance tradeoff is structural, not incidental.
- **A 3Ps positioning lever** — if the moat is owning your context, the consulting wedge is **helping a client build and keep their own context layer** (the [[ai-operating-system]] / [[karpathy-llm-wiki]] deliverable) rather than renting it back from a frontier lab.
