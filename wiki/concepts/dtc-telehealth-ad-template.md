# DTC Telehealth Ad Template

## What it is

The structural pattern Hims uses across all three creative wedges (GLP-1, hair-loss "Hair Hybrids", Sex Rx) in 2026 FB/IG ads. Repeats with high fidelity — every static-narrative Hims ad in [[ads-digest-2026-05-06]] follows the same skeleton, only the wedge-specific bullets and disclaimer change.

## The skeleton

```
[Hook line — outcome or product reveal]

Why [Brand]?
[bullet 1] — Outcome / equivalence claim
[bullet 2] — Provider trust signal
[bullet 3] — Free shipping / discreet delivery

[Disclaimer block — see [[compounded-drug-disclaimer]]]
```

## Cross-wedge invariants

1. **Hook line is always 1-2 sentences** — outcome-focused or product-reveal-focused, never feature-focused
2. **"Why [Brand]?" header transitions to bullet block** — the same three-letter word "Why" anchors the structure across all wedges
3. **Three bullets, always emoji-prefixed** — visual-density compromise: emojis make the bullets scannable on mobile, three is the max before banner blindness
4. **Bullet 3 is shipping/access** — the universal third bullet across wedges, varying only between "Free shipping" and "Free shipping + discreet delivery" depending on stigma category
5. **Closing line is short** — "Your goals. Your plan. Your pace." / "See if you qualify today." / "Get started today—100% online with a free consultation."
6. **Disclaimer block follows** — the [[compounded-drug-disclaimer]] template

## Why the template works

- **Three-bullet hard ceiling** — fits inside the 125-character FB ad-creative-truncation threshold and stays scannable on mobile feed
- **Equivalence + provider + shipping** — covers the three legitimacy concerns of compounded/prescription DTC: *does it work?* (equivalence), *can I trust who's prescribing?* (provider), *will it actually arrive?* (shipping)
- **Hook → bullets → close → disclaimer** — separates persuasion (top) from compliance (bottom) so compliance language doesn't cannibalize hook attention

## Adjacent patterns

- **Hampton Founders' "narrative + proof" structure** ([[entities/hampton-founders]]) — premium B2B variant, no bullet block, narrative paragraphs with embedded proof points; works for high-ticket emotional-decision purchases
- **OpenAI / Anthropic catalog-only carousels** — opposite end of the spectrum; product-feed-driven dynamic creative with zero static template
