# Portable Judgment

## What it is

[[nate-b-jones]]' **26th named framework**, from *Microsoft Says 86% Treat AI Output as a Starting Point. Your Resume Just Stopped Working.* ([[youtube-digest-apify-2026-06-02]] #1, 23.1K views, 2026-05-31, 10:34). The **career-evidence framework** for the AI age — how a worker proves judgment when polished output no longer carries signal.

**The framing claim**: *"The common story is that AI makes us more productive — but the reality is that AI makes **everyone look productive**, and the old career evidence is **losing signal**."*

## The unlock stat (chapter 0:00)

**Microsoft says 86% of workers treat AI output as a starting point**, not a finished product. The implication is the framework's foundation: if nearly everyone is editing AI drafts into polished artifacts, then **a polished artifact no longer proves the person understood the work.** The artifact-as-evidence link — the basis of resumes, portfolios, and work samples — is broken.

> *"Your resume just stopped working."* The resume/portfolio is a record of **outputs**, and outputs are now cheap to produce and impossible to attribute to genuine comprehension.

## The age of whiteboards (chapter 1:20)

The reframe: the AI age is **the age of whiteboards**. A whiteboard conversation — reasoning out loud, under pressure, in real time — **reveals real judgment** in a way a finished artifact cannot. You can't fake live reasoning the way you can fake a polished deliverable.

- **Why standard portfolio advice falls short** (3:50) — "build a portfolio" assumes the artifact proves the skill. When artifacts are AI-generated starting points, the portfolio proves nothing about judgment.
- **The old evidence stopped carrying signal** (2:45) — the entire category of *output-as-proof* lost its information value.

## The four questions (chapter 5:10)

The framework's operational core: **four questions that make your thinking portable and shareable.** They convert tacit, in-the-moment judgment into evidence that *travels* — beyond a single conversation, a single role, or a single employer's systems.

> ⚠️ The **specific four questions** are gated to Nate's Substack post (*"Full Post w/ Prompts and TalentBoard link"*, `natesnewsletter.substack.com/...`) and are not in the digest. Pull the post to fill them in. The *function* is clear from the chapters: capture reasoning in a form that is portable (survives leaving a role) and shareable (legible to others).

## Comprehension over generation (chapter 7:30)

The durable signal is whether you **comprehend** the work — not whether you can **generate** an artifact. Generation is commoditized by AI; comprehension is not. This is the worker-side restatement of the broader vault theme that **judgment is the scarce skill** (cf. [[software-abundance-pm]]'s "market judgment is the new scarce thing", [[ai-operating-system]]'s bike-method "operator judgment").

- **How whiteboard sessions reveal how you learn** (6:38) — the session shows your *learning process*, which is the comprehension signal employers now need.
- **Starting strong in a new role** (8:10) — applying portable-judgment evidence when you join somewhere new (you arrive with reasoning artifacts, not just a resume).

## Preserve the evidence before your badge stops working (chapters 9:25–9:55)

The practical, slightly urgent close: **capture your reasoning artifacts before you lose access.** Your "whiteboard" output lives in systems your employer controls; when your badge stops working (you leave), that evidence is gone.

- **Making reasoning visible without a physical board** (9:25) — how to produce whiteboard-equivalent evidence digitally/asynchronously, so it's portable.
- **The evidence people actually need now** (9:55) — the closing statement of *what counts* as career evidence in 2026: visible reasoning, not polished output.

## The [[prove-it-economy]] connection

Portable Judgment is the **career/hiring-layer extension** of [[prove-it-economy]]'s "prove-it economy for individuals" chapter:

| Framework | Layer | The proof that wins |
|---|---|---|
| [[prove-it-economy]] (14th) | Brand / market — agents interpret you | Verifiable artifacts + public reasoning + reputation portability |
| **Portable Judgment (26th)** | Career / hiring — humans interpret you | Visible reasoning + comprehension evidence (whiteboards), not polished output |

Both reject **claimed credentials / unverifiable narratives** (resume bullets) in favor of **demonstrated reasoning**. Prove-it-economy was triggered by *AI agents* fact-checking brands; Portable Judgment is triggered by *AI-generated output* hollowing out the resume. Same "prove it, don't claim it" core, two different interpreters (agents vs hiring humans).

Also adjacent to [[public-ai-work]] (his 22nd framework) — both are about **making invisible reasoning visible**: public-ai-work for *org apprenticeship* (juniors watch seniors), portable-judgment for *career evidence* (you prove your own judgment).

## Strategic significance

1. **26th [[nate-b-jones]] framework** — first dedicated **career-evidence / hiring-side** framework (prior worker-side frameworks T/C/L/D and the prove-it-economy individual chapter touched it; this names it).
2. **"Output no longer proves comprehension" is a portable 3Ps positioning claim** — the same logic applies to *consultant* signaling: a polished AI-built deliverable doesn't prove the consultant understood the client's problem. The defensible signal is visible reasoning (the [[karpathy-llm-wiki]] / wiki-log audit trail is exactly this — reasoning made durable and inspectable).
3. **Diagnostic-ready like his other frameworks** — the four questions double as a self-audit ("is my judgment portable / shareable?") and a hiring filter ("can this candidate reason on a whiteboard, or only ship AI output?").
4. **Connects to the vault's own architecture** — "preserve the evidence" + "make reasoning visible without a physical board" is precisely what this wiki does (every ingest leaves a reasoned, cited, inspectable trail). The framework names the *career-value* reason to do context-farming, not just the *productivity* reason.

## Why it matters for 3Ps

- **Hiring / talent deliverable** — the framework is a ready-made rubric for evaluating AI-era hires (can they reason live, or only produce output?). Adjacent to [[chief-ai-officer]] and [[prove-it-economy]]'s marketing-role hiring diagnostic.
- **Personal-brand guardrail** — for the consultant/operator persona, the lesson is *build a visible reasoning trail* (public writing, recorded whiteboards, inspectable decision logs), not just a portfolio of polished artifacts.
- **"Capture before badge stops working"** — an operational nudge for any engagement: instrument the reasoning artifacts (decision logs, the wiki itself) so judgment is portable across roles and clients.
