# Claude Wealth Wave (New Generation of Millionaires)

## Definition
[[nate-herk]]'s **opportunity-narrative** framing for the wealth being created *right now* by small, AI-native operators building on [[claude-code|Claude]] — often **without writing code**. Not a tool tutorial; a *"the window is open, here's why and where to start"* pitch. Surfaced via *How Claude is Creating a New Generation of Millionaires* (31.6K views, 2026-07-03, 9:36) in [[sources/youtube-digest-apify-2026-07-04]].

## Key claims
- **The Vulcan story** (chapter 1:49) — a **three-person team winning a state contract**; the case-study proof that small AI-native teams now beat legacy incumbents on real, high-value procurement. The disruption-side wedge shared with [[ai-consulting]] and [[mid-market-ai-agency]] (small boutique out-delivers the slow incumbent). *(from [[sources/youtube-digest-apify-2026-07-04]])*
- **"The four things"** (chapter 2:56) — a named four-part framework for *why Claude specifically* is minting operators. The descriptive core of the concept; the four items are gated to the video (transcript would resolve). *(from [[sources/youtube-digest-apify-2026-07-04]])*
- **Founders running whole companies without writing code** — the no-code-operator thesis; the same *"we don't ever write a single line of code"* throughline as Herk's 712.5K-view *Build & Sell with Claude Code* course. *(from [[sources/youtube-digest-apify-2026-07-04]])*
- **"The window is closing fast"** — an urgency/early-mover close; the register that ties this to his other opportunity framings.
