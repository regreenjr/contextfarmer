### #2 Nate B Jones — *I Stopped Prompting AI One Task At A Time. This Works Better.*
**22.2K, 2026-06-24, 15:38. → New concept: [[loop-of-loops]] (33rd framework).** *"AI agents get useful when you stop prompting one task at a time and start building loops."* A **loop** = *"a recurring job that remembers, notices what changed, and stops where your judgment matters"*; a **loop of loops** composes sub-loops that **notice each other and hand off what changed** (the **school-trip** demo, 3:03); **agents are "loop managers"** (2:17). The **prompt → loop → loop of loops** ladder (1:31) argues most people are stuck prompting one task at a time and the leverage is one or two rungs up. Apps *"left the hard part to you"* (4:06) — the recurring orchestration — which is why a week *"feels heavier than it should."* The guardrail: loops only help if **each has clear boundaries and stops where your judgment still matters.** On-ramp: spot your first loop (5:04) → start with sales/research loops (6:33) → the boring loops like kids' logistics (8:57). → New concept: [[loop-of-loops]]. Updates: [[agent-loops]] (demand-side complement), [[anticipation-gap]] (recurring-job stop condition), [[agent-ownership]] (same-week sibling), [[nate-b-jones]] (33rd framework).
