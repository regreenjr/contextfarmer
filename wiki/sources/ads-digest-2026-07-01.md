## The OpenAI ChatGPT customer-story ad (batch 27's headline)

- ID `899392599869488`, started **2026-06-28**, format **image**, no headline. Body (abridged):
  > *"For James Costello, demolition has always been about understanding how things come together before taking them apart. As a structural engineer overseeing some of New York City's most complex high-rise demolition projects… he is also growing DEMTEC, a demolition business built from his family's roots in the industry. But running a business means managing much more than what happens on site — from estimating and contracts to compliance documents and construction plans… Today, James uses ChatGPT to help navigate that complexity. From reviewing lengthy contracts and identifying important details to organizing documentation and creating construction plans, ChatGPT helps him move through critical workflows faster and with more confidence…"*

**Why it matters**:

1. **A third distinct copy motion — and the softest/most-human of the three.** Batch 9's copy ad was a one-line Codex product-trial CTA; batch 21's was a citation-dense o3 Deep Research research-proof. This one is a **warm customer story** — a named real person, his family business, and concrete everyday workflows (contracts, compliance docs, construction plans). It sells ChatGPT-as-work-copilot through relatable narrative, not a claim or a CTA.
2. **First ChatGPT (flagship product) narrative + first testimonial in the farm.** Prior copy ads promoted Codex (dev CLI) and o3 Deep Research (research reasoning). This promotes **ChatGPT for small business / prosumers** — a different, much larger surface. Across 27 batches no brand had run a customer-testimonial ad before.
3. **Each OpenAI copy ad is a one-off on a different persona.** Codex→developers, o3→clinicians/researchers, ChatGPT→small-business owners. None has been sustained into a multi-ad campaign — OpenAI runs a persistent catalog-placeholder base with sporadic single-shot narrative experiments across product surfaces. → [[openai-narrative-ad-experiments]].
4. **Direct relevance to the 3Ps AI-consulting thesis.** A demolition-contractor-uses-ChatGPT-to-run-his-business story is exactly the small-business AI-adoption narrative the [[ai-consulting]] practice sells. Worth a teardown as a template for "AI for the trades / SMB owner" positioning.

**Open**: is the ChatGPT customer-story ad a one-off (like Codex + o3) or the opening of a sustained customer-testimonial campaign? Watch batches 28-29 for a second customer story.
