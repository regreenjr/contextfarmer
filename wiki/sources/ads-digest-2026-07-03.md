## The o3 re-launch — OpenAI's first copy-ad re-fire

Batch 24 confirmed the batch-21 o3 Deep Research narrative a **one-off** (three batches — 22 revert + 23 silence + 24 catalog-only return — without recurrence, per farm methodology). Batches 25-28 held that reading. **Batch 29 breaks it:**

| | Batch-21 original | Batch-29 re-launch |
|---|---|---|
| Ad ID | `869733176204909` | `1035665692374194` |
| Started | 2026-06-18 | 2026-06-30 |
| Format | image, no headline | image, no headline |
| Body | Boston Children's + Harvard / 376 cases / 18 diagnoses | **identical (verbatim)** |

Three readings, in descending likelihood:

1. **The Hims motion arrives at OpenAI.** Hims re-launches identical creative under fresh ad IDs continuously (25 Hair Hybrids instances and counting). One verbatim re-fire 12 days after the original's start date is exactly what a re-launch machine's first tick looks like.
2. **Campaign refresh / flight re-boot** — the original flight ended and the creative was re-trafficked as a new ad object; behaviorally indistinguishable from (1) at farm resolution.
3. **Sustained o3 research-proof campaign opening** — if batches 30-31 add *more* o3/clinical creatives (new siblings, not just re-fires), the one-off reading inverts entirely.

**Methodology lesson (the farm's 3rd broken closure call):** "structurally closed" was broken twice on Hims wedges (Wegovy batch 14, the diagnosis downgrade to "dormant") — and now **"confirmed one-off" breaks on OpenAI narrative creative**. The standing rule generalizes: **no creative is closed, only dormant**. Any future one-off confirmation (including the pending ChatGPT-testimonial call) is provisional against verbatim re-fire.

The ChatGPT SMB testimonial itself does NOT repeat this batch (2nd consecutive read without recurrence, 28+29).

## The Hims Sex Rx expansion — SKU #5, and the first non-compounded rung

Full copy of the new ad (ID `1019239010591690`, started 2026-06-30, format unknown, no headline):

> *"Have a better sex life—through the ED treatment trusted by millions. Access Generic for Viagra® today, 100% online (if prescribed)*
>
> *Why Hims?*
> *✅ Prescription-strength ingredients*
> *✅ No office visit required*
> *✅ Range of treatment options*
>
> *Prescription products require an online consultation with a healthcare provider who will determine if a prescription is appropriate. Restrictions apply. See website for full details and important safety information.*
>
> *Sildenafil is the generic version of Viagra®. Viagra® is a registered trademark of Viatris Specialty LLC, a Viatris Company. Hims, Inc. is not affiliated with or endorsed by Viatris Specialty LLC."*

What's new:

1. **A 5th Sex Rx SKU — plain generic sildenafil.** The four known SKUs (3-in-1 Pill, Climax Control, Testosterone Support, Hard Mints) are ALL compounded. This is the **first non-compounded Sex Rx creative in 29 batches** — an FDA-approved generic, so the [[compounded-drug-disclaimer]] block **drops entirely** (consultation boilerplate + [[hims]]' standard trademark non-affiliation pattern only, here for Viatris rather than Novo/Lilly/Viatris-Cialis).
2. **A NEW ✅-bullet variant of the [[dtc-telehealth-ad-template]].** Same "Why Hims?" three-bullet skeleton, but uniform ✅ checkmarks replace the semantic-emoji set (🏅🧑‍⚕️📦), and the bullet content rotates to *ingredients-strength → access channel → treatment range*. The "No office visit required" bullet — previously the Hard Mints **fourth**-bullet innovation for high-objection ED — is promoted into the three-bullet core.
3. **The SKU ladder extends downward.** The established wedge-laddering read (each SKU addresses a failure mode: baseline → PE → low-T → non-responders) gains a **price-entry rung below the ladder**: trusted-generic ED at presumably the lowest price point, sold on *"trusted by millions"* mass-proof rather than a compounded differentiator.

Sex Rx had been silent 6 batches (23-28; last instance batch-22 Climax Control). Its return with a *new* SKU rather than a re-launch of an existing one is the first Sex Rx SKU addition since Hard Mints (batch 2, 2026-05-10).
