# FB Ads Digest — 2026-06-24

Twenty-first batch from the [[competitor-ads-farm]]. **49 new ads, 153 dedup-skipped** out of 202 fetched (75.7% dedup) at a **15-day gap** from batch 20 (2026-06-09) — **by far the largest fetch gap in the farm** (prior max was the 6-day gap of batch 16; the farm cron evidently paused ~2 weeks). Apify `facebook-ads-library-scraper` actor. **28 of 49 new ads are tracked-brand signal (57.1%)** — Hims 13 + OpenAI 12 + Ro 3. **Read everything per-day-normalized**: a 15-day gap collapses ~2 weeks of continuous ad-shipping into one fetch, so raw volume massively overstates cadence.
