### #3 Nate B Jones — *Cheap software made your PM job harder, not easier. Here's the new job.* (25th framework)

**11.4K views, 2026-05-29, 12:38. → New concept: [[software-abundance-pm]]. Updates: [[nate-b-jones]] (25th framework), [[ai-consulting]] (adjacent), [[work-primitive]].**

His **25th named framework** — the **product-management-role** framework for the cheap-software era. Signature reframe: *"the common story is that PMs just become prototypers — but the reality is more complicated. Prototyping is table stakes. The real shift is that AI moved the bottleneck, and product management is becoming the discipline that **classifies software abundance into market value**."*

**Chapter map**:
- 0:00 Product management in the age of AI

- 3:45 Where the old PM filter breaks down
- 5:20 Broad building meets governance risk

**Core claims**:
- **The artifact arrives before the request** — somebody (or some agent) already built the thing. The old PM question (*should I build this?*) is replaced by a sharper one: *somebody already built it — should the company actually rely on it?*
- **Microsoft already has 1M+ internal Power Platform assets** (1:14) — the named reality check. Software abundance is already here inside large orgs; the **prototype commons** creates hidden demand *and* hidden risk.
- **The non-technical PM is running out of room** (2:30) — prototyping is table stakes; a PM who can't build *or* govern has no filter left.
- **A production class ladder** (described in the post) — a tiered model for how much a company should *rely on* a given piece of cheap software. **Demotion matters as much as promotion** — moving an asset *down* the ladder (deprecate, sandbox, stop relying on) is now a first-class PM act, not just shipping more.
- **Market judgment is the new scarce thing** (6:40) — when building is cheap, the durable skill is classifying *which* of the abundance actually creates market value.

**Substack monetization**: *"Full Post w/ Prompts"* (`natesnewsletter.substack.com/...`) gates the production-class-ladder spec.

**Why it matters**: extends his cadence to **25 frameworks in 26 days** (T/C/L/D 2026-05-04 → [[software-abundance-pm]] 2026-05-29). This is his first **role-redefinition** framework aimed at the **product-management** function specifically — the PM-side counterpart to T/C/L/D (worker-side) and [[capital-allocation-framework]] (the *should-we-build/buy* decision). The "artifact arrives before the request" reframe is the consumer-of-abundance mirror of his [[work-primitive]] (producer-of-action) framework. → New concept: [[software-abundance-pm]].
