Seventeenth batch from the [[competitor-ads-farm]]. **6 new ads, 192 dedup-skipped** out of 198 fetched at a **1-day gap** from batch 16 (2026-06-05). Apify `facebook-ads-library-scraper` actor.

## TL;DR

- **This is the clean 1-day-gap control batch that RESOLVES the batch-16 central open question** — *"Does Hims sustain copy at a true ~3 ads/day on the next 1-day-gap batch, or revert to a thin placeholder trough?"* **Answer: it reverts.** Hims ships **3 ads = 3.0/day total but only 1 copy ad (Hair Hybrids) + 2 placeholders**; Wegovy and BOTH Sex Rx SKUs (Testosterone Support + Climax Control) — all live in batch 16 — go SILENT. The controlled before/after is decisive: **batch 16 (6-day gap) = 18 ads / 8 copy across all wedges vs batch 17 (1-day gap) = 3 ads / 1 copy Hair-Hybrids-only.** The "mega three-wedge surge" was **6 days of verbatim re-launch accumulation surfaced in one fetch, not an intensification** — exactly as diagnosed. At a true 1-day gap, Hims's copy rate is **~1 copy ad/day (Hair Hybrids standing template)**.
- **The sampling-artifact refinement (batch 16) is now confirmed by a clean controlled comparison.** Same underlying ~3 ads/day rate (mixing wedges + placeholders); a 6-day sample looks like a mega-surge, a 1-day sample looks like a thin Hair-Hybrids-only trough. **All surge/trough reads must gap-normalize first** — this batch is the cleanest demonstration in the farm of why.
