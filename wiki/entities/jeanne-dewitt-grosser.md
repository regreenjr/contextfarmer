# Jeanne DeWitt Grosser

## What it is
Operator-executive currently serving as **COO at Vercel** — oversees marketing, sales, customer success, revenue operations, and field engineering. Built **Stripe's early sales organization from the ground up**. Prior tenure at **Google**. Advises founders on GTM strategy.

- X: `x.com/jdewitt29`

## Why it matters for this wiki
She's the **canonical 2026 GTM voice** for AI-native startups. Her framings on [[gtm-2026]] — the GTM engineer role, build-vs-buy in the AI era, pain-driven buying — are the most-cited GTM heuristics in this wiki's source set. Anchors any 3Ps GTM playbook content.

## Key claims (from [[lenny-rachitsky]]'s podcast in [[youtube-digest-2026-05-03-r3]] #5)

- **GTM is becoming MORE strategically important in the AI era, not less** — counter-intuitive given automation/agent narratives
- **The GTM engineer is the new shape** — fuses sales/marketing ops with software engineering; technical enough to ship internal tooling, GTM-fluent enough to design the workflow
- **Segmentation is foundational** — most teams skip it; her primer is structural advice on how to do it right
- **Sales orgs need to be respected by engineering and product** — designable goal, not an accident; specific habits earn the respect
- **Build vs buy has shifted** in the AI era — what used to be "buy from a vendor" is increasingly cheap to build; the calculus is different than 2 years ago
- **Most customers buy to avoid pain rather than to gain upside** — pricing/positioning/messaging implications cascade from this
