# Ramin J. Imani

## What it is

YouTube channel: **Ramin J. Imani**. Career-strategy creator focused on top-tier consulting (McKinsey, BCG, Bain, Deloitte). Operates "Career Compass Institute" + a free masterclass funnel called "The Obvious Hire Method" (`careercompass.institute/break...`).

## Why he matters for this wiki

He's the **first MBB-insider, top-tier-firm-career voice** tracked in this vault. The existing eight [[ai-consulting]] voices ([[nick-saraev]], [[nate-herk]], [[mark-kashef]], Nick Tan, [[brock-mesarich]], [[ben-ai]], [[mert-yerlikaya]], [[nicole-mccain]]) all sit on the **disruption side** of the consulting wedge — "legacy consulting is dying; solo with AI wins."

Ramin sits on the **survival side**: "legacy consulting *generic* layer is dying; the human-skill stack inside the firm is durable, and the right hire profile becomes more valuable, not less."

Both views can be true; the wedge has two sides. Ramin gives this vault the missing top-end voice.

## Convergence with [[nate-b-jones]]' axis-2 (this batch)

[[nate-b-jones]] in #2 of this batch ([[agentic-implementation-layer]] framework) names **"consultancies moving up the stack"** as one of four axes squeezing generic enterprise AI. Ramin describes the same shift from inside MBB:

- **Outside view (Nate)**: "Consultancies are moving up the stack from advice into agent operations"
- **Inside view (Ramin)**: "MBB and Deloitte are internally restructuring around AI"

Both agree:

- **The middle (generic-implementation consulting) is being squeezed**
- **The high-end (strategic / human-judgment consulting) survives or strengthens**
- **The substrate is shifting** — Nate sees it as the implementation layer; Ramin sees it as the human-skill stack

They diverge on the survival path:

- **Ramin**: build the human-skill stack inside an existing top firm
- **Nate (implicit)**: build implementation-layer primitives (workflow / data access / authority / evals / audit trails)

For 3Ps, both paths exist; the user's positioning is the implementation-layer primitive specialist (Nate's path), not the human-skill MBB-hire (Ramin's path). But Ramin is **useful counter-data** that the consulting category is bifurcating, not collapsing — and the high-end is durable.
