# Y Combinator

Seed-stage accelerator and investor. The YouTube channel publishes long-form founder content including the **Office Hours** format — partners answering real founder questions in panel format. Office Hours episodes function as the canon for "what YC thinks" on whatever topic the questions cover.

- YouTube channel: `youtube.com/@ycombinator`
- Web: `ycombinator.com`

## Why it matters for this wiki

YC partners' aggregated takes on AI-era startup tradeoffs are the highest-credibility public-domain founder advice on the topic. When YC weighs in on AI GTM / pivoting / defensibility, it reflects pattern-matching across hundreds of recent batches — meaning their answers are calibrated against the most current AI-startup reality, not 2010s SaaS lore.

This is **founder-advice canon** for the [[gtm-2026]] frame and a useful counterweight to creator-economy [[ai-consulting]] voices: where Nate Herk / Mark Kashef speak as solo operators, YC speaks as the institutional pattern-matcher.
