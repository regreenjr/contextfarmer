# Ro

## What it is

Ro Health (formerly Roman Health Ventures) — DTC telehealth platform. Founded 2017 by Zachariah Reitano, Saman Rahmanian, and Rob Schutz. Started as men's ED (Roman), expanded to women's wellness (Rory), smoking cessation (Zero), and weight loss (Ro Body). Direct competitor to [[hims]] in the same compounded / branded-prescription telehealth lane.

## Why it matters for this wiki

Ro is the **second-named direct competitor for [[medvi-positioning]]** after Hims. Tracked by the [[competitor-ads]] farm specifically for compounded-GLP-1 creative and pricing.
