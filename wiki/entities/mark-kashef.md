# Mark Kashef

## What it is
Person + YouTube channel running **Early AIdopters** community (`skool.com/earlyaidopters`) and the **Prompt Advisers** AI-automation agency (NLP specialization). Self-described **Data Science Manager by day, AI agency owner by night, ~a decade in AI, based in Canada**. Publishes macro-trend videos arguing solo AI consultants will outpace legacy consulting firms.

## Why it matters for this wiki
He provides the **macro thesis** for the [[ai-consulting]] wedge — "the consulting knowledge moat has collapsed, tacit knowledge is democratized, solos can outpace BCG/McKinsey." Useful as the high-level positioning frame; complements [[nick-saraev]]'s framework-first tactical content and [[nate-herk]]'s operating-systems engineering content.

## Key claims (from video #21 *2026 Will Be The Golden Age of AI Consulting*)

- **Three pillars** shaping the 2026 consulting gold rush (specifics truncated in source description, but framework lives at the start of the video)
- **Knowledge moat collapsed** — what used to be 5 years of accumulated tacit consultant knowledge is now retrievable + applicable in days
- **Tacit knowledge democratized** — AI lets solos research as fast as a McKinsey team
- **Asset delivery in days, not months** — the speed/leverage shift
- **Reusable industry-specific systems compound** — implies the "build vertical SaaS-flavored consulting offerings" thesis
- **Opportunity matrix**: AI strategy, digital transformation, SOP generation, workflow automation, prompt engineering, RAG, niche specialization
