# Ankit Pathak

## What it is

CEO of **ConsultAdd Inc**, an IT-consulting + AI-solutions company with **1,000+ employees** and offices across the U.S. and India. Career arc: **marketing trainee → CEO**, scaling the firm from ~5 to 1,000+ people. Interviewed by [[matt-laker]] in [[youtube-digest-apify-2026-06-30]] (#1, *How Ankit Pathak Built a 1,000+ Person AI Consulting Empire*, 157 views, 40:06).

## Why it matters for this wiki

The vault's first **already-scaled** ([[ai-consulting]]) operator. Most tracked consulting voices are solo/small-agency ([[nicole-mccain]], [[mert-yerlikaya]], [[ramin-imani]]) or agency-scale *aspirants* ([[devin-kearns]] / [[mid-market-ai-agency]], $100M target). ConsultAdd is **operating at 1,000+ people now** — a real-world existence proof of the top of the tier ladder.

### Durable claims (chapter-mapped)

- **"Solve real problems, not sell services"** — the core positioning.
- **"Don't pitch, show results"** (03:00) — prototypes, not decks, won the first clients. Mirrors [[alpine-systems]]' same-batch "entry wedge" (assessment/prototype first) and [[nate-herk]]'s [[ai-operating-system-offer]] sell-hours-then-build ladder.
- **"AI augmenting humans, not replacing them"** — his replacement-anxiety counter-narrative.
- **AI agents for personalized outreach & lead generation** (07:30).
- **Trust via roundtables + ecosystem partnerships** (10:30) — community convening over cold sales.
- **Consultative sales: listen, prototype, solve fast** (14:33).
- **Time-to-Value as the North Star metric** (22:05) — RevOps + customer success organized around speed-to-value, driving expansion (24:42).
- **Flat structure + culture across US/India** (28:20).

The **time-to-value North Star** and **show-results-first** wedge are the most directly portable into 3Ps client diagnostics.
