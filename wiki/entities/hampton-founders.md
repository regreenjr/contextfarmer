# Hampton (Hampton Founders)

## What it is

Hampton — invite-only founder peer-group community founded by Sam Parr (co-host of *My First Million*, founder of The Hustle, sold to HubSpot). 1,000+ vetted founders & CEOs meeting monthly in city-based small groups. ICP is **$3M+ revenue founders** dealing with the "bigger stuff" — co-founder splits, sell vs hold decisions, life/business balance — not tactics.

## Copy patterns to steal

The active static ad is a textbook **emotional B2B founder hook**:

1. **Specific ICP filter as opener** — "Most founders making $3M+ have the same problem" — qualifies the reader and creates self-identification in line one
2. **Reframe the problem as emotional, not tactical** — "they feel stuck often. And not always just on tactics. On the bigger stuff." — separates Hampton from the dozens of "tactics" communities
3. **Three concrete proof points** — "1,000+ vetted founders," "monthly," "in your city" — quantified social proof + concrete cadence + concrete geographic hook
4. **Three example topics that mirror buyer's actual pain** — "co-founder splits, whether to sell, how to balance life and business" — proves the community talks about what the buyer is actually wrestling with
5. **Soft CTA** — "Interested in joining?" — low-pressure, invite-coded

This is the canonical premium-founder-community copy template. Direct steal candidate for [[ai-consulting]] community-pitch landing pages.
