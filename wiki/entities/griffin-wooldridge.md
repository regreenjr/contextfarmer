# Griffin Wooldridge

## What it is

YouTube channel **Griffin Wooldridge** — a **product/UX designer** and AI-tools creator (`griffinwooldridge.com`, `x.com/GriffinWDesigns`, business `griffin@griffinwooldridge.com`). His content sits in the **design-native** corner of the AI-creator economy — how a working designer uses AI tools inside a real design workflow, rather than the developer or make-money-online framing that dominates the rest of this vault's farm. Enters the vault via [[youtube-digest-apify-2026-07-05]].

## Why it matters for this wiki

His entry video, *How to Use Claude Skills as a Designer* (**241,333 views**, 2026-03-09), is the **highest-view single-vertical Claude-Skills video** tracked in the vault — a mainstream-reach signal that [[claude-skills]] crossed into the **design-creator** audience. It is the design-vertical analog to [[grace-leung]]'s marketing angle: instead of "which skills to install" (curation) or "how to author one" (authoring), Griffin shows a **coherent end-to-end pipeline of five design skills** used together. → new concept: [[design-skills]].
