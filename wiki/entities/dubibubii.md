# Dubibubii

YouTube channel **Dubibubii**. Mid-tier AI tooling creator; format leans on viral-tool roundups and curated install lists ("I Spent 48 Hours Testing Viral AI Tools" linked from the digest video).

## Why they're in this vault

Surfaced in [[youtube-digest-apify-2026-05-05]] #5 with *The Only Claude Skills You Need in 2026* (78.9K views, 2026-04-08). Sits in the [[claude-skills]] curation tier between [[nate-herk]] (47K on his "100+ tested") and [[brock-mesarich]] (134.9K on his 15-skill bundle).

## Curation philosophy

**Mixed Skills + MCPs + repos**, not pure Skills. This is a deliberate blend rather than the pure-Skills curation that [[nate-herk]] and [[brock-mesarich]] favor. Audience framing in the description: "vibe coders, solo founders, or just trying to ship faster."

## Notable surfaces

- **`karpathy/autoresearch`** — first surfacing in this vault; a [[andrej-karpathy]] project distinct from his LLM Wiki gist. Open question whether this is the production form of the [[karpathy-llm-wiki]] pattern.
- **"500,000 skills on the market right now, and 95% of them are completely useless"** — first explicit market-size claim for the Skills ecosystem in this vault. Worth verifying; if accurate, the curation-video format will only get more important.
- **Cross-curator overlap**: Frontend Design + Superpowers appear in [[nate-herk]] #25 ([[youtube-digest-apify-2026-05-03]]) and (likely) [[brock-mesarich]]'s 15-skill bundle. Cross-curator consensus is forming around a small core of "must-install" skills.
