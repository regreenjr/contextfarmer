# Nick Saraev

## What it is
Person + YouTube channel **Nick Saraev**. Runs **Maker School** community ("get customer #1 guaranteed"). Publishes multi-hour course-format videos on Claude Code, Antigravity, agentic workflows, and N8N.

## Why it matters for this wiki
He's one of the four creators in [[youtube-digest-apify-2026-05-03]] explicitly positioning around **[[ai-consulting]]** as a 2026 wedge. His framing is the most "consultant-first" of the four — actual consulting frameworks (driver trees, FAST, business acumen, communication) layered onto AI tooling.

## Key claims (from video #7 *The Four Key AI Consulting Basics*)

- **AI consulting is consulting first, AI second** — apply structured consulting frameworks to real business goals
- **Four key tools**:
  1. Driver trees (clarifying goals and drivers)
  2. Business acumen (focusing on profit, growth, risk)
  3. Communication (clear, problem-first messaging)
  4. FAST framework (systematic decision making)
- **Tech alone is not the differentiator** — the framework discipline is

This is the cleanest "professionalize AI consulting" pitch in the digest. Contrasts with [[mark-kashef]]'s "golden age" macro framing and [[nate-herk]]'s "operating systems" tactical framing.

## 5-pillar blueprint (from #3 *How I Would Start AI Consulting in 2026*)

Customer-journey decomposition with copy-paste templates for every stage:

1. **Marketing** — Upwork profiles, cold email formulas, content templates
2. **Sales** — sales call scripts, discovery decks
3. **Onboarding** — client intake SOPs
4. **Fulfillment** — automation templates, full workflow library
5. **Retention** — macros, replies, ongoing-engagement templates

Claims agencies have scaled to **$25K/month and beyond** using the pattern. Includes "all macros, replies, & templates" linked from the description (Google Doc).

This pattern maps **directly** to a 3Ps services menu: any client engagement covering AI-augmented GTM operations needs all five pillars. See [[ai-consulting]] and [[gtm-2026]] for the cross-cutting framing.
