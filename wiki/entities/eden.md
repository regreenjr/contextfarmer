# Eden (TryEden)

## What it is

Eden (tryeden.com) — DTC telehealth platform in the **compounded GLP-1 / weight-loss** lane. The "Eden" brand the [[competitor-ads-farm]] Set 2 (GLP-1 telehealth / Medvi competitive set) was configured to track alongside [[hims]], [[ro]], and [[henry-meds]] — explicitly *not* the gardening / bedding / fashion / skincare brands the bare-keyword search returns.

> Note: Eden's full product line, pricing, and compounded-vs-branded GLP-1 mix are unconfirmed in this vault — the only observed datapoint so far is a single catalog-driven placeholder ad. Details below are flagged where unverified.

## Why it matters for this wiki

Eden is a **direct competitor for [[medvi-positioning]]** in the compounded-GLP-1 telehealth category — same playbook as Hims/Ro (100%-online consult → prescription → ship-to-door), tracked specifically for compounded-GLP-1 creative, disclaimer language, and pricing structure. Until batch 13 it was a configured-but-absent tracked brand; the first real ad confirms Eden is active on Meta paid social and now feeds the creative-test backlog once it ships teardown-able copy.
