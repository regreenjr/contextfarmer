# Dan Martell

YouTube channel **Dan Martell**. SaaS operator turned coach; trained "thousands of entrepreneurs," claims to have built companies doing $100M+/year. Author of *Buy Back Your Time*. Publishes the **Martell Method** newsletter.

## Why he's in this vault

Surfaced in [[youtube-digest-apify-2026-05-05]] #2 with *How to Get SO Many Customers with AI it feels ILLEGAL* (285K views, 2025-10-10). His content is a **non-Claude operator counterpoint** — useful precisely because his audience does not yet route through the Anthropic stack.
