## The daily-driver curation sub-format

Zinho introduces a distinct curation sub-pattern alongside the existing voices:

| Curator | Format | Views | Selection criterion | Audience |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[nate-herk]] | "100+ tested, these 6 are best" | 47K | Best-of-N from large sample | Practitioner |
| [[brock-mesarich]] | "15 I can't live without" | 134.9K | Curated essentials bundle | Non-technical |
| [[dubibubii]] | "33 you actually need" | 78.9K | Mixed Skills + MCPs + repos | Stack-curator |
| **[[zinho-automates]]** | **"9 I use every single day"** | **13.6K** | **Daily-driver / frequency-of-use** | **Practitioner** |

The **daily-driver framing** differs from the existing formats:

- **Smaller N** — 9 vs 6 vs 15 vs 33 (smallest of the four)
- **Frequency-based selection** — what gets opened every day, not what's most powerful
- **"Steal Them" framing** — explicit copy-paste invitation; lower-friction onboarding
- **Less aspirational** — "here's what works for me" vs "here's what's best"

For new Claude users, daily-driver lists may be the **lowest-friction first step** — install these 9, start there. The other formats either ask for evaluation (best-of-N) or commitment (essentials bundle).
