It pulls the prospect's LinkedIn, work history, company size, recent news, open roles and buying signals so I walk into every call prepared. In this video I take myself completely out of the loop and automate the whole thing inside my AIOS, so the research fires the instant a booking is made and the notes land on the calendar invite before I've even opened my laptop.
